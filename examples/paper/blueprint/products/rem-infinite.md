For an arbitrary family $(X_\alpha)_{\alpha \in A}$ the statement is
Tychonoff's theorem, which needs the axiom of choice; the argument above
only gives finite products, by induction on the number of factors:
$\prod_{k=1}^{n+1} X_k \cong \bigl(\prod_{k=1}^{n} X_k\bigr) \times X_{n+1}$.
