We prove that a space is compact exactly when every family of closed sets
with the finite intersection property has a point in common
([thm-fip]), and use it to show that a product of two compact spaces is
compact ([thm-product]).  The argument is the one in Čech's and Erdős'
lecture notes, written so that every step can be checked on its own.

Throughout, $X$ and $Y$ are topological spaces and $\Opens{X}$ is the set of
open subsets of $X$.
