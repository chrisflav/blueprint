An *open cover* of $X$ is a family $\fam{U} = (U_i)_{i \in I}$ of open sets
with

$$
\bigcup_{i \in I} U_i = X .
$$

A *subcover* is a subfamily $(U_j)_{j \in J}$, $J \subseteq I$, that is
still a cover; it is *finite* when $J$ is.
