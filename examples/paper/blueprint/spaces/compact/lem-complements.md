For a family $(F_i)_{i \in I}$ of closed sets put $U_i = X \setminus F_i$.
Then
$$
\bigcap_{i \in I} F_i = \emptyset
\iff
\bigcup_{i \in I} U_i = X ,
$$
so $(U_i)$ is an open cover exactly when the $F_i$ have no common point.
